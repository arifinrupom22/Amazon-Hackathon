## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the intersection of rigorous engineering discipline and domestic warmth—defined as "Warm Architectural Precision." Designed for busy households, discerning families, and productivity-oriented individuals, the interface balances conversational AI autonomy with deliberate human oversight.

The visual ethos rejects sterile tech minimalism in favor of structured warmth. Interfaces feel composed, tactile, and grounded: reminiscent of high-end architectural stationery, bespoke Scandinavian interior planning, and professional agentic tools like Linear. 

The emotional tone is calm, capable, and discreet. Visual weight relies on meticulous alignment, balanced typographic cadence, and restrained borders rather than high-contrast ornamentation. Surfaces feel layered and soft, instilling trust when orchestrating daily family schedules, maintenance roadmaps, and automated domestic errands.

## Colors

The palette establishes an atmosphere of domestic tranquility bolstered by authoritative operational clarity:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Canvas (`#F7F6F2`): A warm ivory backdrop that eliminates harsh screen glare and provides an organic foundation.
  - Primary Surface (`#FFFFFF`): Pure white reserved for interactive cards, flyouts, and active input panels to create natural focal contrast.
  - Secondary Surface (`#FAF9F6`): A subtle off-white for secondary cards, sidebar panels, and nested modules.
- **Brand & Interaction:**
  - Primary Accent (`#177C75`): A deep, mature teal that anchors primary calls to action, selected active states, and AI agent execution badges.
  - Subtle Accent Surface (`#E5F3EE`): A pale mint wash used for contextual agent highlights, active schedule slots, and badge backdrops.
- **Typography & Structure:**
  - Deep Ink Navy (`#17212B`): The primary text color, delivering dense readability without the harshness of pure black.
  - Muted Slate (`#707985`): Secondary and tertiary text token for metadata, system status, and microcopy.
  - Neutral Warm Gray (`#E6E7E3`): The standard structural border and hairline divider, keeping separation crisp and tactile.
- **Feedback & Semantics:**
  - Warning (`#D97706` text/border on `#FEF3C7` background): Warm amber for scheduling conflicts and urgent maintenance alerts.
  - Restrained Error (`#DC2626` text/border on `#FEE2E2` background): Soft crimson for failed agent automations and critical action confirmations.

## Typography

Geist serves as the unified typographic engine across all hierarchy levels. Its engineered proportions, neutral grotesque geometry, and refined tabular figures provide the crisp utility of developer environments while pairing harmoniously with soft domestic surfaces.

- **Display & Headlines:** Tightly tracked headings (-0.03em to -0.015em) provide structural weight and executive clarity for dashboard sections and AI plan titles.
- **Body:** Calibrated line heights (1.4–1.5) maintain sustained comfort during extended reading of conversational agent reasoning, schedule summaries, and vendor notes.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** High-precision, tabular-friendly labels support operational metadata (timestamps, task states, estimated durations, and member assignments). All monetary amounts and time values should use tabular lining figures.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a strict 8pt spatial grid, scaling in 4px increments for micro-alignments (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 32px).

### Grid & Structure
- **Desktop (1200px+):** A 12-column fluid grid framed with `margin-desktop` (40px) and `gutter-desktop` (24px). The layout supports a 3-column paradigm:
  1. Navigation and household contexts (240px fixed).
  2. Agentic conversation and primary planner workspace (flexible 6-8 columns).
  3. Contextual inspector or timeline schedule (320px–380px fixed).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** An 8-column layout with `margin-tablet` (24px) and `gutter` (16px). The contextual inspector transitions to an overlay drawer; conversational threads and planner views share prime real estate.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** A single-column vertical stack with `margin` (16px) and `gutter` (16px). Task timelines and multi-step plan cards collapse into vertically expandable accordion cards. Bottom navigation anchors navigation and instant agent prompting.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of low-contrast outlines and atmospheric ambient diffusion. High-elevation drops and drop shadows with black tinting are avoided; all shadows use warm navy undertones to simulate natural daylight filtering across an ivory workspace.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the main `#F7F6F2` canvas. Non-interactive backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Grounded Cards & Panels):** Surface `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF9F6` framed with a 1px solid `#E6E7E3` border. Accompanied by an ultra-subtle ambient lift: `0px 1px 3px rgba(23, 33, 43, 0.04), 0px 1px 2px rgba(23, 33, 43, 0.02)`. Used for task rows, day schedule cells, and sidebar cards.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Modules & Multi-Step AI Plans):** 1px solid `#E6E7E3` border with `0px 4px 12px rgba(23, 33, 43, 0.06), 0px 1px 3px rgba(23, 33, 43, 0.03)`. Used for actionable AI proposition cards, hovering task cards, and conversational prompt bars.
- **Level 3 (Overlays, Context Menus & Popovers):** 1px solid `#E6E7E3` with `0px 12px 28px rgba(23, 33, 43, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px rgba(23, 33, 43, 0.04)`. Used for contextual member assigners, date pickers, and modal confirmations.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined roundedness tier (0.5rem / 8px base) that strikes the balance between mechanical precision and household warmth:

- **Standard Components (`rounded` = 8px):** Input fields, inline buttons, dropdown triggers, and status badges.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg` = 16px):** Schedule timeline containers, agent workflow cards, multi-step proposal blocks, and slide-out sheets.
- **Overlays & Dialogs (`rounded-xl` = 24px):** Primary modal dialogs and onboarding surfaces.
- **Avatars & Filter Pills:** Fully circular / pill shapes for member avatars, category toggles, and autonomous status indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#177C75`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none, border-radius 8px, height 36px (desktop) / 42px (mobile). Hover state: `#13635E` with subtle ambient shadow. Focus state: 2px ring offset with `#177C75`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Background `#FFFFFF` with 1px solid `#E6E7E3`, text `#17212B`. Hover state: background `#FAF9F6`, border `#707985`.
- **Agent Action (Autonomous):** Background `#E5F3EE`, text `#177C75`, 1px solid rgba(23, 124, 117, 0.2). Displays Lucide icons (14px) with trailing action status.

### Input Fields & Conversational Prompt Bar
- **Prompt Bar:** Persistent floating bar on `#FFFFFF` with 16px border-radius, 1px solid `#E6E7E3`, and Level 2 elevation. Contains embedded accessory actions (family member tagger, document attach, schedule picker) and an execution button.
- **Standard Input:** Single-line and textarea fields use `#FFFFFF` background, `#17212B` text, `#707985` placeholder, 1px solid `#E6E7E3`, 8px radius. On focus: border becomes `#177C75` accompanied by an ambient 2px outer glow (`rgba(23, 124, 117, 0.12)`).

### AI Plan Cards (Multi-Step Execution)
- Structured on `#FFFFFF` with a 16px radius, Level 1 elevation, and a distinct left status indicator rule (2px solid `#177C75` for in-progress; `#D97706` for needs-review).
- Sub-tasks utilize sequential step indicators (16px circular badge with label-sm tabular font), step descriptions in body-md, and inline member assignments.

### Task Rows & Schedule Timelines
- **Task Rows:** Height 44px, border-bottom 1px solid `#E6E7E3`, background transparent. Hover reveals quick-action controls (reschedule, assign, delegate to AI). Checked state applies strike-through and shifts label color to `#707985`.
- **Timeline Slots:** 64px vertical hour blocks bordered with hairline `#E6E7E3`. Event blocks use pale mint (`#E5F3EE`) or ivory tint surfaces with a 4px left accent bar in `#177C75`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Compact 26px height, 8px padding horizontal. Default state: background `#FAF9F6`, 1px solid `#E6E7E3`, text `#707985`, label-md font. Selected state: background `#E5F3EE`, border `#177C75`, text `#177C75`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom 16x16px boxes with 4px border-radius. Unchecked: 1.5px border `#707985` on `#FFFFFF`. Checked: solid `#177C75` fill with white checkmark icon. Smooth 150ms ease-out scale transition on selection.
